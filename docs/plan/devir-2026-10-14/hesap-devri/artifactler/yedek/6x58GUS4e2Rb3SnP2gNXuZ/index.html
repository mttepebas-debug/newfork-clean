Features

Everything between “who’s on tonight?” and a full house.

One tool for the schedule, the requests and the conversations around it.

Drag-and-drop weekly schedule

Build the week in a grid that reads like your floor. Drafts stay private until you publish.

Conflicts before they happen

Availability, approved time off, overlaps and rest gaps are flagged the moment you place a shift.

Swaps and pickups, approved up the chain

Crew trade shifts among people who are actually eligible. The right manager approves in one tap.

Built for more than one location

Each location keeps its own manager. Shifts someone works elsewhere show as ghosts, so nobody gets double-booked.

Time off without the text thread

Requests land with the person who decides. Approved days block the grid automatically.

Announcements people acknowledge

Post the new menu or the health inspection date and see exactly who has read it.

Documents and e-signatures

Collect certifications and signed policies during onboarding, with expiry reminders built in.

Templates and exports

Save a good week as a template. Print or share a clean PDF for the back-of-house wall.

Roles that match your restaurant

Owner, admin, location manager and crew each see what they need, and nothing they should not.

How it works

From sign-up to a published week in one afternoon.

  1. 01

    Create your workspace

    Add your locations and the jobs you staff: server, line, host, bar.

  2. 02

    Invite your team

    Send invites by email. People join from their phone in under two minutes.

  3. 03

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is Newfork for?

Restaurants in the United States, from a single location to groups of a few locations with a manager at each one and an owner overseeing them all.

Do my employees need to download anything?

No. The crew app runs in the phone's browser. They sign in with the invite you send and see their shifts, requests and announcements right away.

Can employees swap or pick up shifts on their own?

They can ask. Newfork only offers a shift to people who work that job at that location, and the request goes to the manager who decides.

What happens if I schedule someone during their time off?

Newfork warns you while you are placing the shift. Availability is always a warning; approved time off is shown in red so it is hard to miss.

Does Newfork do payroll or time clocks?

Not today. Newfork focuses on scheduling and team operations so it does that job well. Time tracking is on our roadmap.

How is my data protected?

Every workspace is isolated at the database level, access follows each person's role, and connections are encrypted. See our Privacy page for details.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Early access is free. Create a workspace, invite a few people and run a real week.
