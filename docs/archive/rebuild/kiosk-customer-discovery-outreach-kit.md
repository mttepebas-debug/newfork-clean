# Kiosk Customer Discovery Outreach Kit

Status: prepared on 2026-08-17. No message has been sent, no participant has been contacted, and no
personal data has been collected.

## Purpose

Provide ready-to-review recruitment, consent, facilitation, and note-handling materials for the
approved kiosk customer-discovery plan. This kit does not authorize outreach or replace the
organization's privacy, research, employment, compensation, or records-retention policies.

## Required Decisions Before Outreach

Assign an accountable research owner and complete all of the following outside the repository:

- approve the customer/contact list and the person authorized to contact each organization,
- select an approved restricted system for contact details, consent records, raw notes, and any
  recordings,
- define retention and deletion dates for raw research material,
- decide whether sessions may be recorded and who may access recordings,
- approve participant compensation, payment handling, and any employer-specific requirements,
- provide a scheduling method and participant support contact,
- define how accessibility, language, time-zone, and communication accommodations will be offered,
- define escalation for accidental disclosure of credentials, employee records, or other sensitive
  information.

Do not put contact details, recordings, raw quotes, consent records, employee records, or
compensation data in this repository.

## Recruitment Principles

- Describe this as research, not a sales call, beta invitation, or promised roadmap.
- Do not say managed-device or offline work is planned for release.
- Recruit employees through a voluntary channel that does not reveal participation or refusal to
  their manager where practical.
- Do not ask managers to select only high performers, technology enthusiasts, or employees with
  known clock issues.
- Do not interview employees about individual attendance, discipline, disputes, or job performance.
- Do not require a disability diagnosis. Offer accommodations without asking participants to
  justify them.
- Keep concept reaction after current-workflow questions.
- Use the same approved compensation policy for comparable participant groups; compensation must
  not depend on favorable feedback or pilot commitment.

## Opening And Consent Script

Read this before substantive questions:

> Thank you for joining. We are researching current shared time-clock workflows and problems before
> making product decisions. This is not a sales call, support case, job-performance review, or
> promise that a feature will be built. The session will take about [duration]. Participation is
> voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Please do not share names of other
> employees, PINs, credentials, time records, disciplinary details, medical information, or security
> secrets. We will use deidentified findings in aggregate. Do you agree to participate?

Record the response in the approved restricted system, not this repository. Do not continue without
affirmative participation consent.

### Separate Recording Permission

Ask only after participation consent:

> May we record audio or video to help with note accuracy? Recording is optional. Declining will not
> affect participation, compensation, or your relationship with Newfork. Access and deletion follow
> the research retention policy described in [approved notice].

Record one of: `recording approved`, `notes only`, or `session declined`. If permission is withdrawn,
stop recording immediately and follow the approved deletion procedure.

## Facilitator Checklist

### Before The Session

- Confirm participant role without copying contact details into repo files.
- Confirm the sample matrix still needs this perspective.
- Prepare a research ID such as `ORG-B / LOC-2 / EMP-1` in the restricted research system.
- Verify consent notice, recording configuration, retention date, and compensation procedure.
- Test meeting accessibility, captions/interpreter support, and time zone.
- Close unrelated customer systems, dashboards, logs, and production credentials.
- Prepare the current-workflow questions before any concept material.

### During The Session

- Read the opening script and collect separate recording permission.
- Ask for the last concrete occurrence before general opinions.
- Capture frequency, impact, workaround, recency, ownership, and counter-evidence.
- Ask what smaller operational or network change could solve the problem.
- Do not diagnose, dispute, sell, promise, troubleshoot production, or collect secrets.
- Stop screen sharing or recording if sensitive data appears.
- Present managed-device and offline concepts separately and only after current-state evidence.
- Distinguish interest, named ownership, device count, success outcome, and actual commitment.

### Immediately After

- Confirm whether any sensitive information was accidentally captured and follow deletion policy.
- Separate observations from researcher interpretation.
- Add contradictory evidence and unanswered questions.
- Assign managed-device/offline scores independently; do not score accessibility or packaging as
  demand.
- Produce a deidentified summary and keep raw material in the restricted system.
- Record whether follow-up is needed without placing contact details in the repository.

## Cancellation And Withdrawal

Participants may cancel or withdraw without explanation. Confirm cancellation without persuasion.
If a participant requests deletion, follow the approved policy for raw notes and recordings and
record completion in the restricted system. A deidentified aggregate that can no longer be linked
back to the participant must be handled according to the approved notice; do not improvise a
promise during the session.

## Research Handoff

At the end of recruitment and interviews, the research owner must provide only:

- the deidentified evidence rows,
- sample coverage and known bias,
- aggregate managed-device and offline scores plus hard-gate evidence,
- accessibility barriers stated as functional outcomes,
- packaging evidence tied to buyer, replaced cost, and measured support expectation,
- pilot commitments referenced by research ID, with real contact details retained outside repo,
- unresolved questions and withdrawals/deletions that affect the usable sample.
